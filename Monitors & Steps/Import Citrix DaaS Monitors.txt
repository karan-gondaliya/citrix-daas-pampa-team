STEP 1 -- Log In to Datadog
-----------------------------
  Log in to your Datadog account.


STEP 2 -- Navigate to Monitors
---------------------------------
  From the left-hand menu, navigate to Monitoring -> Monitors -> New Monitor.
  (Or go directly to: Monitors -> New Monitor)


STEP 3 -- Click "Import From JSON"
-------------------------------------
  On the New Monitor page, click the "Import From JSON" button in the
  top-right corner.


STEP 4 -- Paste the Monitor JSON
------------------------------------
  1. Open the desired JSON file, copy its full contents.
  2. Paste the contents into the "Paste your JSON monitor definition" box.


STEP 5 -- Save the Monitor
------------------------------
  Click "Save" to create the imported monitor.


STEP 6 -- Repeat for Each Monitor
-------------------------------------
  Repeat Steps 2 to 5 for each of the nine JSON files listed above.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NOTE
----
  Make sure your Datadog account has the necessary permissions to create
  and edit monitors.
